ParleHub offers four plans designed to match where your firm is in its AI adoption journey — from a small team piloting shared AI conversations to a fully governed, unlimited-seat deployment with your own storage, identity infrastructure and model deployments. All plans give you per-project budgets, expense codes, and multi-provider model access (BYOK). The tiers differ in seat and project capacity, storage, SSO, own-storage integration, bring-your-own model deployments, and organization knowledge.

Every monthly plan you can buy today starts with a **7-day free trial** — there is no separate Free plan. Try ParleHub on a real engagement before you commit to anything.

## Understanding model spend

Model spend is the cost of the AI API calls that ParleHub routes through your connected provider keys. When you send a message in a project conversation, ParleHub calls the model on your behalf using the API key you supplied in **Organization Settings → Model access**, records the token counts and cost against the project's budget, and passes the charge on to your provider account.

**Team plan:** Your entire organization shares a \$10/mo model spend cap across all projects and seats. Once that cap is reached, new messages are blocked until the following month. This is intentionally enough to run a meaningful pilot on one or two engagements.

**Business, Business Elite and Enterprise plans:** Model spend is uncapped within ParleHub. You pay your model provider directly through your own API keys (Bring Your Own Key). ParleHub enforces the per-project budgets you set and the org-wide and personal caps you configure, but it does not add a markup or impose a ceiling above those limits.

<Note>
  **Plan prices, the Team plan's model spend cap, the credit wallet and your ParleHub subscription are all in US dollars.** Your own provider usage is different: ParleHub can record it in the currency your provider bills you in, cap projects in USD, GBP, EUR or CAD, and report in the currency you choose. See [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies).
</Note>

### **Managed Models**

These are models that you can use that ParleHub is hosting. This allows you to try models that you don't have a relationship with, or open models you don't want to host yourself. The cost of these models is deducted from your Credit Wallet, which is always held in US dollars. Managed models are available on every plan, including Team, and are unaffected by the bring-your-own Foundry deployment requirement above — that requirement applies only to connecting a model deployed in your own Azure subscription.

## What happens if your plan changes

If your organization moves to a plan with fewer features, or goes over a limit on your current plan:

- **You keep your data.** Nothing is deleted because a feature is no longer included or a limit has been reached.
- **Features outside your plan stop working for new use, but what you already have stays visible.** You can still view and remove what you created with a feature your plan no longer includes.
- **Private projects stay private.** A restricted project never becomes visible to the rest of the organization just because the plan changed.
- **Going over a limit blocks adding more, not what is already there.** For example, if you are over your seat or storage limit, you cannot add another seat or file until you are back under it, but nobody is removed and nothing already stored is deleted.
- **Losing Integrations stops adding and using them, not what you already connected.** You cannot connect a new integration or count on the agent calling one it already had, but every integration you configured stays visible, and anyone who connected their own account can still view and disconnect it. See [Choosing who the credential belongs to](/tools/integration-scopes#if-your-plan-changes).

<Note>
  If your organization was on the previous Free plan, you have a grace period to choose a paid plan — see your organization's **Settings → Billing** for the exact date, and [Managing Members](/admin/managing-members) if you need to reduce seats first.
</Note>
