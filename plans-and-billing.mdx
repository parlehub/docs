ParleHub offers three plans designed to match where your firm is in its AI adoption journey — from a no-commitment pilot on a single engagement to a fully governed, unlimited-seat deployment with your own storage and identity infrastructure. All plans give you per-project budgets, expense codes, and multi-provider model access. The tiers differ in capacity, SSO, storage integration, and the model spend ceiling.

## Understanding model spend

Model spend is the cost of the AI API calls that ParleHub routes through your connected provider keys. When you send a message in a project conversation, ParleHub calls the model on your behalf using the API key you supplied in **Settings > Model Providers**, records the token counts and cost against the project's budget, and passes the charge on to your provider account.

**Free plan:** Your entire organization shares a $10/mo model spend cap across all projects and seats. Once that cap is reached, new messages are blocked until the following month. This is intentionally enough to run a meaningful pilot on one or two engagements without any financial commitment.

**Team plan:** Model spend is uncapped within ParleHub. You pay your model provider directly through your own API keys (Bring Your Own Key). ParleHub enforces the per-project budgets you set and the org-wide and personal caps you configure, but it does not add a markup or impose a ceiling above those limits.

**Enterprise plan:** Same as Team — uncapped through BYOK, with all the per-project and org-level enforcement tools — plus dedicated support to help you structure budgets, configure storage, and roll out across your firm.

### **Managed Models**

These are models that you can use that ParleHub is hosting. This allows you to try models that you don't have a relationship with, or open models you don't want to host yourself. The cost of these models is deducted from your Credit Wallet.
