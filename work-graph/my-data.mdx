Parlé's Project IQ keeps a record of the work your organization brings into projects: meetings, decisions, action items, documents and who was involved. Every member can see what it holds about them, in one place, and act on it.

<Info>
	Every member can open this page, at any time. It still works if your
	organization's plan no longer includes Project IQ, or an admin has switched
	Project IQ off.
</Info>

## See what Parlé holds about you

Open your account menu and select **What Parlé knows about me**.

The page has five lists:

- **Projects**: the projects you are on, with your role and the date you joined.
- **Meetings I attended**: newest first.
- **Decisions and action items**: where you are a decider or the owner.
- **Documents I authored**: documents you wrote or edited.
- **People I've worked with**: names only, with the meetings you shared. There are no counts and no ranking of anyone.

Each row says what it is, when, where it came from ("from your connected account", "shared by someone else", "a project meeting") and **why it is on your page** (for example "you attended" or "you are the owner"). It links to the meeting or project when you can open it.

### What you may not see here

- **Only what you could open at the source.** The page shows only items you could open yourself. It says how many more items mention you that you cannot open, and suggests asking your Org Admin for a subject-access request.
- **Entries may be missing.** When Parlé has guessed that an entry is you but a reviewer has not confirmed it, the page says "Some entries may not be linked to you yet, so they are not shown here."
- **Long lists.** A long section shows its newest entries.

## Flag something as disputed

If a meeting, document, decision or action item is wrong, is attributed to you but is not yours, or is sensitive, you can flag it. A flagged item is hidden from Parlé's agents until someone reviews it.

<Steps>
	<Step title="Find the item">
		On **What Parlé knows about me**, or on the item itself: the meeting page, the
		decisions and actions list, the task list or the project timeline.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Select Flag as disputed…">
		Choose a reason. Reasons are fixed choices; you do not write anything:
		- **It's wrong**: what Parlé holds misstates what happened.
		- **It isn't mine**: it's attributed to you, but it isn't yours.
		- **It's sensitive**: it shouldn't be repeated.
		- **Something else**: another reason it shouldn't be used.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Select Flag as disputed">
		Parlé says: "Parlé's agents won't use it until someone reviews it. People who can see it still will, marked Disputed."
	</Step>
</Steps>

What a flag does:

- **Agents stop using it, for everyone.** No agent tool, brief, content search or Research Task returns or counts it, including for you. Parlé also stops summarizing it and suggesting from it.
- **People who could already see it still can.** It shows a **Disputed** badge. Nobody sees who flagged it.
- **A reviewer decides.** Whoever may forget the item reviews it in the **Disputed** list, on the project's **Review** tab or under **Org settings → Disputed**. They can **Clear** the flag, which puts it back with agents straight away, or **Forget…** the item.
- **You are told.** You are notified when the flag is cleared ("Your disputed flag was cleared") or the item is removed ("What you flagged was removed from Parlé").
- **You can take it back.** Select **Withdraw my flag** at any time. Only the person who raised a flag can see that button.
- **You can have 20 open flags** at a time.

On your own page, people and project rows have no flag button. For your own data, the answer is to forget it.

## Forget what is yours

Forgetting removes **Parlé's record**. The email, file or meeting at its source is never touched.

<Steps>
	<Step title="Forget one item">
		On an item that came from your own connected account, select **Forget this**.
		Parlé shows what would be removed, by count. Type **forget** and select
		**Forget this**.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Or forget everything about you">
		At the foot of the page, select **Forget everything about me**. Type **forget
		me** to confirm. This cannot be undone.
	</Step>
</Steps>

- **Project records show a note instead.** Meetings and records that belong to a project show "A project admin can remove this from the project." Something that came from someone else's account says that only they or an Org Admin can remove it.
- **What stays.** Your account, and the records you own in projects, stay. Forgetting everything removes what Project IQ holds about you, even items your organization's data controls are currently hiding.
- **What is not reached.** A forget does not reach replies an agent already wrote, or another meeting's transcript that names you.
- **It stays forgotten.** Parlé will not bring the item back from a later sync. See [Project IQ data controls](/admin/work-graph-data-controls#forgotten-items-are-not-re-imported).

## When Project IQ is off or not in your plan

- **Switched off by an admin.** The page says "Project IQ is switched off". What Parlé holds about you is hidden from this page and everywhere else, but it is still held. You can still forget everything about you.
- **Plan without Project IQ.** The page says "Your plan no longer includes Project IQ". Parlé keeps what it holds, and this page and forgetting still work. Nothing new is added until the plan includes it again.

## Notifications

Project IQ notifications appear in the bell and on the **Notifications** page, labeled by kind:

- **Meeting reminder**: Parlé asks you to add a transcript or notes for a meeting you attended, with the reason, such as Teams transcription being off for it.
- **Request**: an item was flagged as disputed and waits on your review, or the outcome of a flag you raised.
- **Task**: a task was assigned to you.
- **Sharing change**: something shared with a project changed.

Dispute notifications name the kind of item, never its title or content.
