sidebarTitle: "Overview"

---

Much of a project's real work never happens in a Parlé conversation. It happens in meetings, in email, in shared folders and in decisions nobody wrote down. The **Work Graph** brings that work into the project so the team, and the project's agents, can see it alongside their conversations.

Everything in the Work Graph belongs to one project. People see only what they are allowed to see in that project, and agents read it through the same permission checks. See [Work Graph permissions](/security/work-graph-permissions) for how that works.

<Note>
	**Availability:** the Work Graph is part of the **Business Elite** and **Enterprise** plans. It is off on Free, Team and Business unless Parlé staff turn it on for your organization. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing).
</Note>

## What it brings in

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Meetings" icon="calendar">
    When a meeting happened, who was in it, and its transcript or notes. Meetings arrive from Teams and Outlook, or you add them by hand.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Decisions and action items" icon="list-check">
    What was decided and who agreed to do what, each pointing back at the part of the transcript it came from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Files and channels" icon="folder-open">
    A Teams channel, or a OneDrive or SharePoint folder, that a member shares with the project.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Forwarded emails" icon="envelope">
    Emails (with their attachments) that members forward to the project's own Parlé email address.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Microsoft 365 is the first connected source. Meetings, transcripts, files and Teams channels come in through each person's own Microsoft 365 work account. See [Connecting sources](/work-graph/connecting-sources).

## Where you see it

- **Meetings** — each meeting has its own page with attendees, transcripts and notes, and the decisions and actions taken in it. See [Meetings](/work-graph/meetings).
- **The project's work views** — the Work tab, timeline, dashboard and review queues show what has come in and what needs a person's attention. See [Project views](/work-graph/project-views).
- **Conversations and agents** — mention a meeting with `@` in a conversation, or start a conversation from a meeting, and the agent can answer from it and cite it. See [Agents and the Work Graph](/work-graph/agents).

## You stay in control

- **Nothing suggested is treated as fact.** Parlé can suggest decisions and action items from a meeting. They stay marked as proposed until a person confirms them.
- **Sharing is deliberate.** A meeting or folder comes into a project because someone shared it, or because its organizer invited the project's address. Parlé never searches a person's whole account.
- **Org Admins and Business Admins have switches.** They can turn the Work Graph, individual sources and groups such as transcripts off. See [Work Graph data controls](/admin/work-graph-data-controls).
- **People can see and manage their own data.** See [My data in the Work Graph](/work-graph/my-data).

If your plan stops including the Work Graph, you keep everything already in Parlé. You can still open, read, remove and delete what is there. You just can't add new items.
