This page is for **everyone**. It covers the questions you can ask the assistant about **your own ParleHub setup and your firm's documents** — the things it used to guess at, and now looks up.

There are two halves, and they work differently:

- **Your live setup** — budgets, models, tools, storage, spend. These change by the hour, so the assistant **queries them** the moment you ask.
- **Your firm's documents** — policies, handbooks, methodology. These are written and change rarely, so the assistant **searches them** and quotes the relevant passage with a citation.

You do not have to know which is which, or switch anything on. Just ask.

## Questions about your setup

Ask these in the conversation where you are already working, rather than leaving to hunt through a settings screen.

### In a project conversation

| Ask something like | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| _"How much of our budget have we used?"_ | This project's monthly and lifetime caps, what has been spent and what remains — read from the authoritative ledger, with each figure's currency stated. |
| *"Who's on this project?"* | The project roster with everyone's role. |
| *"Which models can we use here?"* | The models actually available in this project — and, if one you expected is missing, why. |
| *"What can you reach from this project?"* | The tools your organization has registered and enabled here, and the file storage this project is connected to. |

### In a personal conversation

| Ask something like | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| *"What has the organization spent this month?"* | Your organization's spend for that period. Works for a calendar month, quarter or year. |

Everything from the project list works here too.

## How to tell it looked something up

Above the answer you will see a line naming what the assistant is doing:

> *Checking the project's budget*  
> *Checking which models are available*  
> *Checking which tools are available here*  
> *Looking up your organisation's spend*  
> *Looking up the project team*

<Warning>
  **That line is the point.** It is what separates a figure the assistant read a second ago from one it half-remembers from earlier in the conversation. If you asked about your budget and no such line appeared, treat the number with suspicion and ask again in a fresh conversation.
</Warning>

## Why some questions are only answered in a personal conversation

This is the part that surprises people, so it is worth understanding rather than working around.

**A project conversation is shared.** Everyone in the project sees it, and one answer goes to all of them. So in a project thread the assistant will only tell you things **every member of that project can already see on screen for themselves**.

Your organization's total spend across every client is not one of those things. So if you ask for it in a project thread, the assistant answers the part it can — this project's budget — and declines the rest. It is not being obtuse, and asking again will not help: the figure would land in every member's view and stay in the thread permanently.

**Ask it in your own personal conversation instead**, where the audience is one person.

### Being on your own does not make you an admin

In a personal conversation the assistant still checks your role. If you are not an **Org Admin**, asking about organization-wide spend gets you **your own** usage for that period — never the firm's. That is the same rule the admin screens already apply; the conversation is not a way around it.

### What the spend figures mean

Spend comes back **split by who it is owed to**, and deliberately never added up:

- **Your own provider keys** — usage billed to you directly by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or whoever holds the account.
- **ParleHub-managed models** — usage drawn from your prepaid credit.

**Every figure names its currency, and says how it was arrived at.** The assistant is handed a finished figure with its three-letter code — for example `£4,230.00 GBP` — and a sentence about its basis, which it quotes rather than working anything out itself. It is never given an exchange rate, so it cannot convert a figure on its own. You will see one of three kinds of sentence:

- **No conversion was needed** — the figures are _"exactly as recorded against your usage — no exchange rate was applied"_.
- **Converted** — the figures were converted at the exchange rate ParleHub published for the month named, or, across several months, each month at its own rate and then added together, with the months listed.
- **Approximated** — some usage was recorded before ParleHub published a rate for that currency, so it was converted at the earliest rate ParleHub holds.

Organization spend is given in your organization's reporting currency. Your prepaid credit balance is the exception: it is held in US dollars and is not converted, and the assistant says so. In a project, if the caps and the spend figure are in different currencies, the assistant gives both but will not subtract one from the other to tell you what "remains" — it says why instead. See [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies).

A single total would mean two different things at once, so you will not be given one. The managed figure is described as an **estimate**: it is worked out by applying today's settings to past usage, so if your access mode or pricing changed mid-period it will not match exactly. **Your credit history is the authoritative record** — see the **Usage** screen for that.

<Note>
  If your organization has personal workspaces switched off, there is no personal conversation to ask in, so organization-wide spend cannot be reached this way at all. Use the **Usage** screen instead.
</Note>

## Questions about your firm's documents

If an Org Admin has set up [organization knowledge](/knowledge/organization-knowledge), you can ask about your firm's written policies in the same breath:

> *"What does our expenses policy say about booking flights for a client visit?"*  
> *"What's our standard notice period in an engagement letter?"*  
> *"Do we have a policy on using client data for training?"*

You will get the relevant passage, followed by a **citation** — the document name and version, as a link you can click to open the document itself.

Three things worth knowing:

- **The citation is trustworthy by construction.** Its label is resolved from ParleHub's own record of the document, not written by the assistant — so it cannot cite one document and link to another, and it cannot invent one.
- **Hover the citation** to see the effective date of the version that was quoted.
- **A retired policy cannot be quoted.** When your firm supersedes a document, the old version comes out of the index — so you will not get a confidently-cited answer from a policy that no longer applies. In an older conversation, a citation to a since-retired document shows as `[document unavailable]` rather than opening.

If nobody has set this up yet, the assistant simply will not have anything to quote. Organization knowledge is available on the **Business Elite plan and above** — point your Org Admin at [Set Up Organization Knowledge](/knowledge/organization-knowledge).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What's happening |
| --- | --- |
| The assistant answers about your budget with no lookup line, and the number looks wrong | It answered from the conversation's own earlier history rather than looking it up. Start a fresh conversation and ask again. |
| It will not tell you organization-wide spend in a project thread | Working as intended — see above. Ask in a personal conversation. |
| It gives you your own usage instead of the organization's | You are not an Org Admin. |
| It does not seem able to look anything up at all | Some models cannot call tools. Switch the conversation to a model that supports them. |
| It cannot find a policy you know exists | Check with your Org Admin that the document is published and **Searchable**, and that your project has not opted out of organization knowledge in **Tools & knowledge**. |
| It says it could not state the figures in your currency and gives US dollars instead | An exchange rate was missing. That is a ParleHub issue, not a problem with your account. Do not compare that answer with figures elsewhere in your own currency — the two differ by an exchange rate. |
| A spend figure does not match your invoice | The managed figure is an estimate derived from current settings. Use the **Usage** screen and your credit history as the record. |
