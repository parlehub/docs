ParleHub is model-agnostic: your organization connects its own API key for each AI provider it wants to use, and ParleHub uses that key to run conversations. This is sometimes called **BYOK** ("bring your own key") or **BYOAK** ("bring your own API key").

Using your own key means:

- **You are billed directly by the provider**, at the provider's own rates — ParleHub does not mark up model usage.
- **Your data stays under your provider account's terms**, not ParleHub's.
- **Your organization controls which models are available** to your team, and can rotate or remove a key at any time.

<Note>
  ParleHub also offers **ParleHub Managed** models that need no key of your own — these are billed from your organization's ParleHub credit wallet instead, always in US dollars. Managed models do not appear in the setup steps below because there is nothing to connect.
</Note>

## Connecting a Provider

Only **Org Admins** can add, rotate, remove, or test a provider key.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Model access settings">
    Go to **Organization Settings → Model access**. Each provider has its own card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your API key">
    Find the provider's card and paste your key into the **API key** field. See the tabs below for where to get one, and what to check on the provider's side first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Connect">
    ParleHub makes a small verification call to the provider before saving, so you find out immediately if the key is wrong rather than at chat time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the quote currency">
    If this provider bills you in a currency other than US dollars, say so now — before any rates or spend are recorded. See [Setting your quote currency](#setting-your-quote-currency) below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What the verification call tells you

| Result | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Key accepted** | The card switches to **Connected**, with the date it was verified. |
| **Key rejected** (invalid, revoked, or wrong account) | The key is **not saved**. You are asked to check it was copied in full and has not been revoked. |
| **Provider unreachable** | The key is saved anyway, so a provider outage does not block you — but marked as **not yet verified**. It is checked again automatically the next time you rotate or test it. |

To replace a key later, select **Rotate key** on a connected card, paste the new value, and select **Save key**. To disconnect a provider entirely, select **Remove** — its models immediately stop being available to your team.

## Setting Your Quote Currency

The **quote currency** is the currency your agreement with a provider is quoted and billed in. ParleHub supports **USD, CAD, EUR and GBP**, and every connected provider card shows a **Quote currency** line. It defaults to **USD**.

Where you set it depends on the provider:

| Provider | Quote currency |
| --- | --- |
| **Anthropic**, **OpenAI** | Fixed at USD. The card reads _"Quote currency: USD — This provider quotes and bills in USD only, so its quote currency cannot be changed."_ |
| **Google Gemini** | Set on the provider card. |
| **Azure AI Foundry** | Set on **each deployment**, not on the credential, because one Azure resource can serve deployments under different agreements. The card reads _"Quote currency: USD — This provider's quote currency is set per deployment below, not on the credential."_ |
| **ParleHub Managed** | Always USD. There is nothing to set. |

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Azure AI Foundry deployment">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Azure AI Foundry card">
        Go to **Organization Settings → Model access** and find **Declared deployments** on the Azure AI Foundry card.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the deployment's Quote currency">
        Each deployment row has its own **Quote currency** list. Pick the currency your Azure agreement bills that deployment in. The change saves as soon as you select it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your rates in that currency">
        Under **Deployment rates**, the form now reads _"Enter prices in GBP, exactly as your price sheet quotes them — ParleHub stores this deployment's rates in GBP."_ Type the figures exactly as they appear on your price sheet. Do not convert them yourself.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **Why it matters.** Every message sent to that deployment is costed from those rates, recorded in that currency, and converted at ParleHub's monthly exchange rate wherever it is measured against a project cap or shown in a report. Each rate is stamped with the currency it was entered in and keeps it, so your rate history still reads correctly if the deployment's currency changes later. See [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies).

    <Warning>
      **If you leave a deployment at USD but type rates from a GBP or EUR price sheet, nothing errors — the figures are simply read as dollars.** Recorded spend is then wrong by the exchange rate, project caps are measured against the wrong amount, and the Foundry reconciliation statement will not match your Azure invoice. Set the currency **before** you enter rates.
    </Warning>

    **Azure list prices are USD only.** For a deployment in any other currency ParleHub does not apply or compare Azure's list prices — the rate card says _"Azure publishes list prices in USD only, so ParleHub does not compare this GBP deployment against them."_ Moving a deployment away from USD also stops any Azure list price ParleHub was applying to it, so the deployment reads as not priced until you enter your own rate card in the new currency. Do both in one sitting.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Gemini credential">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Google Gemini card">
        Go to **Organization Settings → Model access**. The card must be **Connected**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the Quote currency">
        Pick a currency from the **Quote currency** list under _"The currency your agreement with this provider is quoted in."_ The change saves as soon as you select it.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Today this is a **declaration**. ParleHub prices Gemini usage from its own USD reference rates, so Gemini costs are still recorded in USD whatever you select here. What the declaration does drive is your organization's **default reporting currency**: if every active credential is quoted in the same currency, reports default to it. See [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies#reporting-currency).
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### When the quote currency can be changed

You can change a quote currency freely **until your organization has a live rate of its own on file under it**. After that the change is refused, so an existing rate can never end up describing a different price sheet from the one it was typed from. The message names the count and the currency, for example:

> _"This deployment has 1 live rate on file in GBP, entered by your organization. Retire it and re-enter the rate card in EUR before changing the quote currency."_

To change it anyway, retire your rate on the deployment's row under **Deployment rates** (**Use list price**, or **Clear estimate** on a provisioned deployment), change the **Quote currency**, then enter the new rate card. Retired rates stay in the rate history in the currency they were entered in, and spend already recorded keeps the currency and rate it was charged at — changing the currency changes nothing that has already happened.

Two more things worth knowing:

- **Rotating a key keeps its quote currency.** Removing a key and adding a new one for the same provider also carries the previous currency over, rather than resetting to USD.
- **Every change is recorded in the audit log**, with the old and new currency.

## Provider-Specific Setup

Each provider has account-side setup you need to do **before** pasting a key into ParleHub. These steps happen on the provider's own website.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Anthropic">
    Create a key in the [Anthropic Console](https://console.anthropic.com) under **API Keys**. Copy it before closing the dialog — Anthropic only shows the full key once.

    Make sure the workspace the key belongs to has billing set up and available credit. A key from a workspace with no credit will connect, but requests will fail.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="OpenAI">
    Create a key in the [OpenAI Platform](https://platform.openai.com) under **API keys**. Project-scoped keys (`sk-proj-…`) are supported and recommended, since they let you limit exactly which models a key can use.

    **Check model access on the key's project before connecting it.** OpenAI enables model access per project, separately from having a valid key — a key can be valid and still be refused for a specific model if that model is not enabled for its project, or if the organization has not completed OpenAI's own account verification for that model. ParleHub surfaces this as **"This organisation's OpenAI account can't use '\<model>'"** rather than a generic key error, so you know to check OpenAI's project settings rather than the key itself.

    Confirm the project has billing configured — an unfunded project will pass key verification but fail on real requests with a quota error.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Gemini">
    Create a key in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com). This uses Google's Gemini Developer API.

    **No Google Cloud project, Vertex AI setup, or service account is needed** — just the API key.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Azure AI Foundry">
    Create (or reuse) an Azure AI Foundry resource in the Azure Portal, and create a **deployment** for each model you want to use. In Azure AI Foundry, the model you pick in ParleHub corresponds to a **deployment name** on your resource, not a raw model name.

    **Check which currency your Azure agreement bills in.** Azure can bill in your local currency, and ParleHub records each deployment's costs in the currency you declare for it. Set each deployment's **Quote currency** before you enter its rates — see [Setting your quote currency](#setting-your-quote-currency).

    <Warning>
      **Bringing your own Azure AI Foundry deployment requires the Business Elite plan or above.** This does not affect ParleHub Managed models, which remain available on every plan. On a plan without it, connecting the resource or declaring a deployment is refused with _"Using your own Azure AI Foundry deployments is not included in your ParleHub plan. Upgrade to enable them…"_
    </Warning>

    Azure AI Foundry has one more step than the other providers — declaring your deployments — and an Org Admin does all of it in **Organization Settings → Model access**. In the app the card is titled **Microsoft Foundry**.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect the resource">
        Enter the **Resource endpoint** of your Foundry resource (for example `https://your-resource.services.ai.azure.com`) and its **API key**, then select **Connect**. A Foundry project is not required. A classic Azure OpenAI resource (`.openai.azure.com`) cannot be connected; Microsoft publishes an in-place upgrade to Foundry.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Declare your deployments">
        ParleHub cannot list the deployments on your resource from the API key alone, so you tell it which ones your team may pick. Under **Declared deployments**, select **Add deployment** and enter the **Deployment name** exactly as it appears in your Foundry resource, the **Model it serves**, the **Deployment type**, the **Region** and the **Context window (tokens)**. **Label** and **Capabilities** are optional — leave a capability off unless you have confirmed the deployment supports it. Select **Declare deployment**. The deployment is available to your team as soon as it is declared.

        A deployment's name cannot be edited afterwards, because requests and past usage resolve through it — retire the deployment and declare it again under the new name.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Optionally, let ParleHub list them for you">
        Under **Pick deployments from Azure**, select **Connect Azure** and grant ParleHub read-only access to that one Azure resource. **Pick from Azure** then lists your deployments with their models and billing types as Azure reports them, so you do not have to type them. **Add by hand** stays available for a deployment Azure has not listed yet.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the quote currency and check the rates">
        Set each deployment's **Quote currency**, then review **Deployment rates**. ParleHub prices most pay-as-you-go deployments from Microsoft's published USD list price, so they are ready to use as soon as you declare them. A pay-as-you-go deployment that shows no rate needs your rates entered before anyone can send to it. A provisioned deployment has no per-token price: it works normally and its usage is reported in tokens with no cost figure, unless you add an allocation estimate. See [Setting your quote currency](#setting-your-quote-currency).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Enabling Models and Setting a Default

Connecting a key does not automatically make every model from that provider available to your team. You choose which models to enable, and one becomes the organization's default model for new conversations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the Available models list">
    On a **Connected** provider card, each row shows the model's name, context window size, and whether it supports tools and vision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the models you want and save">
    Select **Save models**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally turn on Auto-allow new models">
    When the provider releases a new model, it is enabled automatically instead of waiting for an admin to opt in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the organization default">
    Next to any enabled model, select **Set default**. The first model you enable becomes the default automatically; you can change it at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Models your provider account cannot actually see — most commonly an OpenAI model not enabled for the connected project — appear **greyed out and unselectable**, with a note explaining they are not available on your account. Enable them on the provider's side first, then revisit this list.

See [Model Selection](/models/model-selection) for restricting which of these models a given project may use.

## Testing a Connection

**Test connection** sends a real, one-word message to the provider using your organization's key and a chosen model — the same code path a real conversation uses. It is the most reliable way to confirm a key and model work end to end, because some provider-side restrictions (like OpenAI's model-access and account-verification gates) only show up on an actual request, not on a simple key check.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Test connection">
    On a **Connected** provider card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the prompt">
    It tells you which model will be used and that this makes a real request on your own provider account, at a fraction of a cent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    **Success** shows the response time in milliseconds. **Failure** shows a plain-language reason plus the provider's own error text, so you can act on it directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Connection tests are not available for **ParleHub Managed** models — there is no API key of your own to test.
</Note>

## What Happens to Your Key

ParleHub stores your API key encrypted at the org level, resolved at request time. The key is never displayed again in the UI — only a masked placeholder appears in settings to confirm a key has been saved. It is used solely to forward API requests on your behalf.

<Warning>
  Only Org Admins can add or remove provider connections. Project admins and members cannot view, copy, or modify API keys in any part of the interface.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key rejected when connecting | Key copied incorrectly, revoked, or from the wrong account | Re-copy the full key (including any prefix) from the provider and try again |
| Card shows "saved, but not verified" | Provider was unreachable at save time | No action needed — it is re-checked on the next rotate or test |
| Model greyed out in the Available models list | Provider account cannot see that model (e.g. not enabled on an OpenAI project) | Enable the model on the provider's side, then refresh the list |
| Test or chat fails: "account can't use this model" | Model not enabled for the key or project, or the provider's own account verification is incomplete | Check the provider's model-access settings for that key or project |
| Test or chat fails: key rejected | Key invalid or revoked since it was added | Rotate the key with a fresh one |
| Test or chat fails: no remaining quota or credit | Provider account billing or credit exhausted | Add billing or credit with the provider — retrying will not help |
| Test or chat fails: rate limited | Too many requests sent to the provider recently | Wait a short while and try again |
| **Test connection** unavailable | Provider is ParleHub Managed | Managed models have no key of your own to test |
| No **Quote currency** list on a card, only a line of text | The provider bills in USD only (Anthropic, OpenAI), or it is Azure AI Foundry, where the currency is set on each deployment | Nothing to do for a USD-only provider. For Foundry, use the **Quote currency** list on the deployment under **Declared deployments** |
| Changing a quote currency is refused: "…live rate on file…" | Your organization has a live rate of its own recorded under the current currency | Retire that rate, change the currency, then re-enter the rate card in the new currency — see [When the quote currency can be changed](#when-the-quote-currency-can-be-changed) |
| A Foundry deployment reads as not priced after a currency change | Azure list prices are USD only, so none applies to a non-USD deployment | Enter your own rates for it under **Deployment rates**, in the deployment's currency |
| Foundry costs look wrong by roughly an exchange rate | Rates from a non-USD price sheet were entered while the deployment was still set to USD | Retire the rate, set the deployment's **Quote currency**, and re-enter the rate card. Spend already recorded keeps the rate it was charged at |
| Azure AI Foundry models not selectable | No deployment has been declared yet, or a pay-as-you-go deployment has no rate | Declare the deployment under **Declared deployments** on the card; if it shows no rate under **Deployment rates**, enter your rates for it |
| Connecting Azure AI Foundry is refused: "…not included in your ParleHub plan" | Your plan does not include your own Azure AI Foundry deployments | Upgrade to Business Elite or above. An existing connection is untouched and can still be removed or rotated |

Still stuck? Contact ParleHub support with your organization name, the provider, and (if shown) the error detail from the connection test.
