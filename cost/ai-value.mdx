The **AI Value** screen estimates how much time Parlé's help saved, what that time is worth to your firm at the rates you set, and how that compares with what the AI cost. It answers the question a client or a finance lead asks next, after you have read your spend on [Usage](/cost/usage-analytics): *what did we get for it?*

<Warning>
	AI Value is an **estimate**, not a measurement, and not a bill. It is built from
	published sources and Parlé's own estimates of how long work takes by hand. It
	does not time anyone, it is never used to charge you, and it is not an invoice
	or a record of what a client paid. Use it to compare periods, projects and kinds
	of work, and to start a conversation, not as an audited figure.
</Warning>

## Where to find it

- **The organization's AI Value screen.** Select **AI Value** in the main sidebar, under Organization and just after **Usage**. Org Admins and Business Admins can open it.
- **A project's AI Value tab.** Every member of a project sees an **AI Value** tab on that project, between **Review** and **Project Setup**. It covers that project's work only.

Both views show a period you pick, by week or by month. Weeks and months follow the time zone shown on the screen. On the organization's screen that is the organization's time zone, otherwise UTC. On a project's tab it is the project's own time zone, otherwise the organization's, otherwise UTC. Weeks start on Monday.

## What the screen shows

| Figure | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Hours saved** | The estimated time the work would have taken by hand, less the time the conversations actually took, added up across tasks. |
| **FTE capacity** | Hours saved as a share of a full-time year. One full-time equivalent is 1,800 working hours a year, so a week is 1/52 of that and a month is 1/12. |
| **Cost saved** | Each task's hours multiplied by your **cost rate** (what an hour of that work costs your firm). |
| **Client value** | Each task's hours multiplied by your **bill rate** (what your firm charges for an hour of that work). |
| **AI spend** | What the AI cost in the same period, from the same ledger as [Usage](/cost/usage-analytics). |
| **Return** | Cost saved and client value, each divided by AI spend. |

The screen also charts hours over time and breaks hours down by type of work and by project.

## What is counted

Parlé counts **tasks**, not messages. A task is one piece of work in a conversation: a draft, a research report, an analysis. The kind of work comes from the same work labels that power **Usage by work type**.

- **One credit per task.** A follow-up question on the same piece of work adds no second credit. Editing and resending a message, or retrying with a stronger model, keeps it one task. Moving on to a different kind of work starts a new task.
- **Size.** Each task is Small, Medium or Large, by how many replies it took on its longest path: one reply is Small, two to four is Medium, five or more is Large. Size comes from the reply count only, never from what was said, so a slow task never earns a bigger estimate.
- **The period.** A task counts in the week or month it began.
- **Personal conversations.** Personal work counts toward the organization's totals, with one protection: when fewer than three people had personal conversations in a period, that period's personal time saved or personal AI spend is left out, and the screen says so, so that no one's own work can be worked out from the totals. Personal work never appears on a project's tab.

A figure that cannot be worked out is shown with a reason, never as a silent zero: **Not set** (no rate), **Not available** (a rate that could not be converted, or spend that could not be priced), **No work this period**, **Left out** (personal work held back, see above) or, on the Return tile, **No spend yet** (return needs AI spend in the period).

## How time is measured

For each task Parlé takes two numbers.

1. **The manual estimate.** The minutes the task would have taken by hand for its type of work and size. These come from Parlé's baseline, or from your own minutes where you have set them.
2. **The time the conversation took.** From the task's first question to its last reply. A pause of more than **10 minutes** between turns is treated as time away from the work and left out, so leaving a conversation open over lunch does not count against it.

The task's saving is the first number less the second, **never below zero**. A task that took longer than the estimate saves nothing; it never subtracts from the total.

### Where the baseline comes from

The baseline minutes are **Parlé's own estimates**. They are referenced to the task statements in the O\*NET® database and to US Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data, which is for reference only. They are not a published dataset of how long tasks take, and they never set your rates. The baseline is versioned: a new version applies to work from then on and never rewrites a figure already worked out. The screen names the baseline version used in the period you are looking at.

## Set your own rates and minutes

The baseline is a starting point. Your firm knows better how long its own work takes and what an hour costs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Go to **Org settings → Organization** and find the **AI value** card for the organization's settings. For one project, open that project's settings and find its **AI value** card. The **AI Value** screen links to the same card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a rate">
    Enter an hourly **cost rate** and **bill rate**. Rates are in the organization's reporting currency, or the project's, which you set under [Currencies](/cost/currencies#reporting-currency). A rate must be above zero, up to 10,000,000, with at most two decimals. A rate that is not set stays unset: Parlé never fills it in from wage data, and the money figures say **Not set** with a link back here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your own minutes">
    For any type of work you can enter your own manual minutes for Small, Medium and Large tasks, from 0 to 2,400 whole minutes. Each field shows, greyed, the figure that applies if you leave it empty: the organization's minutes where it has set its own (on a project's card), otherwise Parlé's baseline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the card. Changes apply to **work started after you save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How the levels combine

Each setting is a default with an optional override:

- **Minutes:** a project's own minutes first, then the organization's, then Parlé's baseline.
- **Rates:** a project's own rate first, then the organization's. If neither is set, the rate is unset.

Setting a value back to what it would inherit removes your override, so the default stays live: if Parlé publishes a new baseline, or the organization changes its figure, the project follows. Each change is recorded in the audit log with its old and new figure.

### Changes are forward-only

A change applies to tasks begun after you save it. Tasks begun earlier keep the rates and minutes in force when they began. A follow-up can still move a task to a larger size, which raises its minutes. Raising a bill rate today therefore does not rewrite last quarter's client value. Because of this, a figure you change can take a period or two to show a real difference.

### Who can change them

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Set the organization's rates and minutes, and any project's they can see | Org Admin or Business Admin |
| Set a project's own rates and minutes | That project's Project Admin |
| View the settings | Everyone else, read-only |

## Plans

AI Value is available on the **Team plan and above**; the Free plan does not include it. The Team plan shows the headline for the current period: **hours saved** (with the number of tasks) and **FTE capacity**. The money figures, earlier periods and the breakdowns by type of work and by project are on the **Business plan and above**. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing).

If an organization leaves a plan that includes AI Value, no new credits are worked out, but the ones already stored are kept. You can still change or clear the rates and minutes you already set; adding new ones needs the plan.

## What these figures are not

- **Not a measurement.** Nobody's time is tracked. The saving is an estimate against a baseline.
- **Not billing.** Nothing here sets what you are charged, and Parlé does not send client value to anyone. It is not an invoice.
- **Not a per-person figure.** The screen shows organization and project totals. It does not show, rank or export what any one person saved.
- **Not an export.** The screen is for reading in Parlé. There is no export of AI Value, and no public API or data feed for it.
- **Not Parlé's wages for your people.** Your rates are yours. The wage data in the baseline is reference only.

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a money figure say Not set?">
    No cost rate or bill rate was in force when those tasks began. Set one in the **AI value** card. It applies to work started after you save; earlier tasks stay unvalued.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a figure not change after I edited a rate?">
    Changes are forward-only. Tasks begun before you saved keep the rates they were valued at.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a period shown as left out?">
    Personal conversations are left out of a period when fewer than three people had them. This protects individuals; it is not an error.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are the figures lower than I expect?">
    A task counts from its first reply, while it is still going, and its figures grow with it. Work Parlé could not classify is counted with no time saved, and time spent beyond the manual estimate counts as no saving. A task takes a short while to appear after a conversation. If you still see nothing after an hour, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
