ParleHub enforces budgets before spend happens, not after. Rather than reviewing an invoice at month-end and discovering a project ran over, every project carries a dollar ceiling that ParleHub checks before a message is sent. Your team works freely up to the budget, and the guardrail kicks in exactly when it needs to, not when the billing cycle closes.

## The Budget tab

The **Budget** tab on a project shows what the project has actually spent — computed from real token usage, not an estimate — broken down per conversation, alongside **Spent**, **Budget** and **Remaining** figures.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Budget tab">
    Navigate to the project and select **Budget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a figure">
    Type the ceiling under **Set budget (USD)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save budget**. The budget becomes active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Set \$0 for no limit.** Spend is still tracked and still attributed to the project — you simply get no hard stop.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Only **Org Admins** can set a project budget. Project Admins and members can view spend.
</Warning>

## How enforcement works

The spend bar on the Budget tab turns **amber at 80%** and **red at 100%**.

**When a project hits its budget, new messages in it are blocked** until an Org Admin raises the limit. A member who cannot change the budget themselves gets a **Request limit increase** button, which notifies an admin. The admin raises the figure on the same **Budget** tab; there is no separate approval queue to work through.

<Tip>
  Set the budget slightly above your expected engagement cost to avoid mid-project interruptions. Raising it later is a one-field change, but it does need an Org Admin to be available.
</Tip>

## Budget layers

Project budgets do not operate in isolation. Each of these can independently stop a send:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Project Budget" icon="folder" href="/projects/overview">
    Scoped to a single project, set by an Org Admin. Stops spend on that engagement.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Personal Budget" icon="user" href="/admin/managing-members">
    A per-user limit that applies across all the projects a member works in, plus their personal conversations.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Org-Wide Cap" icon="building" href="/admin/org-caps">
    The outermost backstop. No single project or member can push total org spend past this ceiling.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A member with personal-budget headroom and project-budget headroom can still be blocked by the org-wide cap, and vice versa.

## Where spend comes from

Spend is recorded per message in an append-only ledger, attributed to **either** the project (for project conversations) **or** the user (for personal conversations) — never both. That is what makes a per-project figure defensible when you take it to a client.

Two kinds of spend are tracked separately and deliberately never added together:

- **Your own provider keys** — usage billed to you directly by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or whoever holds the account. See [Connecting Providers](/models/connecting-providers).
- **ParleHub-managed models** — usage drawn from your prepaid credit wallet.

A single combined total would mean two different things at once, so ParleHub does not present one. The managed figure is an **estimate**, worked out by applying current settings to past usage; your credit history on the **Usage** screen is the authoritative record.

<Tip>
  Anyone on a project can ask the assistant _"How much of our budget have we used?"_ mid-conversation and get the figure read from the ledger. See [Asking About Your Org](/knowledge/asking-about-your-org).
</Tip>
