ParleHub enforces budgets before spend happens, not after. Rather than reviewing an invoice at month-end and discovering a project ran over, every project can carry spending caps that ParleHub checks before a message is sent. Your team works freely up to the cap, and the guardrail kicks in exactly when it needs to, not when the billing cycle closes.

## Two caps, in a currency you choose

A project's budget is **two independent caps**, stated in the project's own **budget currency** (USD, CAD, EUR or GBP):

- a **Monthly cap**, which resets at the start of each calendar month, and
- a **Lifetime cap**, which counts everything the project has ever spent.

You can switch on either, both, or neither, and each has its own amount. Neither implies the other: a monthly cap alone paces spend with no ceiling on the total, and a lifetime cap alone bounds the total with no pacing. **Reaching either one blocks new messages.**

<Warning>
  Only **Org Admins** can set, switch on, switch off or change a cap, or choose the budget currency. Project Admins and members see the caps and the spend against them, read-only, under the line _"Read-only — only an organization admin can change the budget."_
</Warning>

## The Budget tab

The **Budget** tab on a project shows what the project has actually spent — computed from real token usage, not an estimate.

- **Budget & spend** — one card per cap, showing `spent of cap used`, a progress bar, the percentage used and the amount remaining, all in the budget currency. The header reads **Limits in GBP** (or whichever currency the project uses). A cap that is switched off reads **No monthly cap** or **No lifetime cap** — never a zero — and a project with both off reads **No limit — tracking spend only**.
- **Total spend (EUR)** — the project's whole spend, in its **reporting currency**, which can differ from the budget currency. The **Reporting currency** control underneath sets a project override — see [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies#a-project-override).
- **Spend by conversation (EUR)** — the same spend broken down per conversation, also in the reporting currency.
- **Consumption (platform services)** — a separate card, shown to Org Admins only, that controls how the project draws on your organization's credit wallet for ParleHub platform services such as OCR and ParleHub Managed models. When its **Billing mode** is **Capped allowance**, it shows a **Cap (USD / month)** field. That cap is unrelated to the monthly and lifetime caps above, and it is **always in US dollars** whatever the project's budget currency is — so a GBP project shows **Limits in GBP** at the top of the tab and a US-dollar cap further down, and both are correct. See [what stays in US dollars](/cost/currencies#what-stays-in-us-dollars).

<Note>
  When the budget currency and the reporting currency differ, the caps and the total are shown as **separate facts**. The total is never subtracted from a cap or turned into a percentage of one — figures in two currencies are not comparable. Each cap's own spent, remaining and percentage figures are worked out in the budget currency, and they are what enforcement uses.
</Note>

## Setting the caps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Budget tab">
    Navigate to the project and select **Budget**. Org Admins see a **Set spending caps** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Budget currency">
    Pick the currency you report this work in. **It is fixed once the project records any spend**, so choose it before the team starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the caps you want and enter amounts">
    Tick **Monthly cap**, **Lifetime cap**, or both, and type an amount in **Monthly cap amount (GBP)** or **Lifetime cap amount (GBP)**. A ticked cap needs an amount above zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save budget**. The caps take effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Leave both caps off for no limit.** Spend is still tracked and still attributed to the project — you simply get no hard stop. A cap you switch off **keeps the amount you typed**, so switching it back on restores it.
</Note>

You can also set one cap when you create the project: the **New project** form asks for a **Budget currency**, a **Cap period** (**Every month** or **Total, for the life of the project**) and an amount. Add the other cap afterwards from the **Budget** tab. See [Projects](/projects/overview#creating-a-project).

### Why the budget currency locks

Once a project has recorded any spend, the **Budget currency** list is disabled with the note _"This project has recorded spend in GBP, so its budget currency can't be changed."_ Re-reading £5,000 as €5,000 would restate a ceiling somebody approved, and converting it would change the number they signed off. If a piece of work needs caps in another currency, set the caps in the current currency or start a new project.

### Projects created before caps had a currency

Nothing about their enforcement changed. A project that had a budget now has a **Lifetime cap in USD** of the same amount, switched on. A project that had no limit has both caps off.

## How enforcement works

Each cap's bar turns **amber at 80%** and **red at 100%**, and ParleHub warns in the conversation from 80% — for example _"This project is at 85% of its monthly cap of £5,000.00."_

**When a project reaches either cap, new messages in it are blocked.** The notice names the cap and its currency, and says who can fix it:

> _"This project's monthly cap of £5,000.00 has been reached for this month. It resets on the 1st of next month. Only an organization admin can change it — use "Request a higher limit" to ask for one."_

A lifetime cap reads the same way without the monthly reset sentence. The **Budget** tab shows _"This project has reached its monthly cap. New messages are blocked until an organization admin raises it."_

### Requesting a higher limit

Anyone on the project — including a Project Admin — can select **Request a higher limit**, in the blocked conversation or on the **Budget** tab, and add a short note explaining why. The request quotes the caps currently in force, in the project's own currency.

An Org Admin answers it from **Requests** in the sidebar: they choose **Which cap** to raise (**Monthly** or **Lifetime**), enter a **New limit (GBP)** in the project's budget currency, and select **Approve & apply** — or reject it with a reason. An approval only raises a cap that is already switched on; switching a cap on or off is done on the **Budget** tab. Both the request and its outcome are recorded in the audit log.

<Tip>
  Set caps slightly above your expected engagement cost to avoid mid-project interruptions. Raising one later is quick, but it does need an Org Admin to be available.
</Tip>

### Budget alerts

Budget warning and budget-exhausted notifications, and their emails, state amounts in the project's budget currency — for example _"£85.00 of £100.00 used (85%)."_ The two caps alert **separately**: the monthly cap's alerts re-arm on the 1st of each month, while the lifetime cap's warning and exhausted alerts each fire once.

## Budget layers

Project caps do not operate in isolation. Each of these can independently stop a send:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Project Caps" icon="folder" href="/projects/overview">
    Monthly and lifetime, scoped to a single project, in the project's budget currency. Set by an Org Admin.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Personal Budget" icon="user" href="/admin/managing-members">
    A per-user limit that applies across all the projects a member works in, plus their personal conversations. Always in USD.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Org-Wide Cap" icon="building" href="/admin/org-caps">
    The outermost backstop. No single project or member can push total org spend past this ceiling. Always in USD.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A member with personal-budget headroom and project-cap headroom can still be blocked by the org-wide cap, and vice versa. Because personal budgets and the org-wide cap stay in US dollars, their notices spell out the code — _"Personal budget of \$100.00 USD for this period would be exceeded."_ — so they are not mistaken for your own currency.

## Where spend comes from

Spend is recorded per message in an append-only ledger, attributed to **either** the project (for project conversations) **or** the user (for personal conversations) — never both. That is what makes a per-project figure defensible when you take it to a client.

Each message's cost is recorded in the currency your provider quoted it in and converted **once**, at ParleHub's exchange rate for the month it was sent. A cap that spans several months adds up each month's spend at that month's rate, so a later rate change never restates what a project has already spent. See [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies#how-conversion-works).

Two kinds of spend are tracked separately and deliberately never added together:

- **Your own provider keys** — usage billed to you directly by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or whoever holds the account, in the currency of your agreement. See [Connecting Providers](/models/connecting-providers).
- **ParleHub-managed models** — usage drawn from your prepaid credit wallet, always in US dollars.

A single combined total would mean two different things at once, so ParleHub does not present one. The managed figure is an **estimate**, worked out by applying current settings to past usage; your credit history on the **Usage** screen is the authoritative record.

Usage that ParleHub could not put a cost on is never shown as zero: it reads **Not priced** or **Cost unavailable**, with a note saying how many tokens the figures exclude. See [what the two labels mean](/cost/currencies#cost-unavailable-is-not-not-priced).

<Tip>
  Anyone on a project can ask the assistant _"How much of our budget have we used?"_ mid-conversation and get the figure read from the ledger, with its currency. See [Asking About Your Org](/knowledge/asking-about-your-org).
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Budget currency** is greyed out | The project has recorded spend | The currency is fixed from the first spend. Set the caps in the current currency, or start a new project |
| No **Set spending caps** panel | You are not an Org Admin | Use **Request a higher limit**, or ask an Org Admin |
| "Enter a monthly cap above £0.00, or switch the monthly cap off." | A ticked cap has no amount | Type an amount, or untick the cap |
| "ParleHub can't currently price budgets in EUR, so this budget can't be saved yet…" | ParleHub has not published an exchange rate for that currency yet — a ParleHub issue, not yours | Try again shortly. Turning a cap off, or changing an amount in the currency the project already uses, still works |
| Messages refused: "ParleHub can't currently convert this model's prices from GBP…" | An exchange rate is missing, so the send cannot be measured against a cap | Try again shortly; contact ParleHub support if it persists |
| **Total spend** is in a different currency from the caps | The project's reporting currency differs from its budget currency | Expected — see [Currencies and Exchange Rates](/cost/currencies) |
