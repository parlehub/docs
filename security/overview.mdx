If your firm signs client security questionnaires, you already know the questions: where does our data go, who can see it, and what record is kept of who did what? Parlé is architected to give you defensible answers — single sign-on tied to your identity provider, project files stored inside your own SharePoint or Google Drive tenant, an append-only audit log enforced at the platform level, and role-based access that limits who can see what down to the project level.

## What Parlé Does Not Do

Understanding the boundaries is just as important as understanding what is included. Parlé does not:

- **Use personal consumer AI accounts.** All model calls run on your own provider API keys, or a managed Parlé model — never through a personal OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google account.
- **Store files outside your tenant (Business and above).** On the Business plan and above, uploaded project files are written directly to your SharePoint or Google Drive. Parlé processes them in transit to the model but does not retain them in Parlé-managed storage.
- **Mix session data across organizations.** Every organization is fully isolated. Conversations, files, members, and usage data are scoped to your org and never visible to another org on the platform.

## Answering the AI Security Questionnaire

Use the answers below when completing client or internal security questionnaires. Each answer names the plan it applies to where that varies by plan.

<Accordion title="Where is client data stored?">
	On the Business plan and above, project files are stored in your own
	SharePoint site or Google Drive tenant — the same environment your firm
	already governs. Parlé processes file content in transit when sending it to
	the AI model, but does not persistently store that content outside your
	tenant. On the Team plan, files are stored in Parlé-managed secure storage.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which AI provider processes our data?">
	The provider you choose and connect via your own API key. Parlé supports
	Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Azure AI Foundry — bringing your own
	Azure AI Foundry deployment requires the Business Elite plan and above
	(managed models are unaffected). You control the provider relationship, the
	key, and — where your provider agreement permits — the data processing terms.
	Parlé never routes your requests through a shared or pooled consumer account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who can access our AI conversations?">
	It depends on the project's visibility. A conversation in a **Restricted**
	project can be opened only by that project's members and by your Org Admins,
	who can open every project in the organization. A conversation in a **Public**
	project can be opened by anyone in your organization. A personal conversation
	is visible only to the person who owns it. SSO-offboarded users have their
	session invalidated and access removed immediately when their account is
	disabled or removed in your identity provider.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Parlé staff read our conversations?">
	No. There is no view in Parlé that allows cross-organization access to your
	conversations. On all plans, conversations are scoped to your organization and
	are not accessible to Parlé staff in the normal course of operations.
</Accordion>
