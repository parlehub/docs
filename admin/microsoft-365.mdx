This guide is for an organization's **IT administrator**. It connects Parle to your Microsoft 365 tenant in two parts:

1. **Single sign-on (SSO)** — let your team sign in to Parle with their corporate Microsoft accounts.
2. **SharePoint storage** — let project agents read and write files in your SharePoint sites.

Do them in this order. SSO comes first because it establishes the link between Parle and your Microsoft tenant that SharePoint then reuses.

<Note>
  **You'll need:** a Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) admin role — **Global Administrator** or **Cloud Application Administrator** — to grant consent, and **SharePoint Administrator** (or site owner) for the storage step. In Parle you must be an **Org Admin**.
</Note>

## Part 1 — Single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID

Your admin grants the Parle application permission to verify your users' identities. After that, anyone in your organization whose email matches your domain can sign in to Parle with their Microsoft account — no separate Parle password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your tenant details">
    In the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com) → **Overview**, copy your **Tenant ID** and note your primary **domain** (e.g. `contoso.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant consent to Parle">
    In Parle, go to **Org settings → Organization → Single sign-on** and click **Connect Microsoft**. You are redirected to Microsoft and asked to consent to Parle signing your users in. Approve it on behalf of your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the connection in Parle">
    Back in **Org settings → Organization → Single sign-on**, enter:

    - **Tenant ID** — from step 1.
    - **Email domain** — the domain your users sign in with (e.g. `contoso.com`). Parle uses this to route matching users into your organization automatically.

    Then toggle **Enable single sign-on** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Sign out, then on the Parle login page enter a corporate email address. A **Sign in with Microsoft** button appears. Complete the Microsoft prompt — you should land back in Parle, signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **New users:** the first time a colleague signs in with Microsoft, Parle creates their account automatically and places them in your organization — as long as their email domain matches and you have an available seat.
</Info>

<Note>
  Single sign-on requires the **Business plan or above**. If your plan is downgraded, Microsoft sign-in stops working at the next login and users fall back to email/password.
</Note>

## Part 2 — SharePoint as project storage

Parle accesses files using its **own application identity**, scoped to **only the specific SharePoint sites you grant** — never your whole tenant. The agent can then read and write project files even when no one is actively online. Deleted files go to your **SharePoint recycle bin**, where your normal retention and recovery rules apply.

<Warning>
  **You'll need the Business plan or above.** SharePoint storage is available on the **Business** plan and above. You must also have completed **Part 1 (single sign-on)** first — SharePoint reuses the Microsoft tenant captured during SSO setup, so there is no tenant to enter here.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable SharePoint for your organization">
    Org Admin, once. In Parle, go to **Org settings → Files & search → SharePoint**, enter your **SharePoint host** (your SharePoint domain, e.g. `contoso.sharepoint.com`), and click **Enable SharePoint**. Until this is done, SharePoint will not appear as an option in any project's storage settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the SharePoint site">
    Copy the URL of the site that will hold a project's files, e.g. `https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/ClientDelivery`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Parle access to that site">
    Because Parle uses least-privilege, site-scoped access, each site must be granted explicitly, once per site, by a **SharePoint Administrator** or **Global Administrator**. See the two methods below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the site to a Parle project">
    Open the project → **Project settings → Storage**, choose **SharePoint**, paste the site URL from step 2, and click **Resolve**. Parle resolves the site and lets you pick the **document library**, then **browse its folders** and click into the one you want the project rooted at. Stay at the library itself to use the whole library. Then **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Parle verifies the connection">
    It lists the folder you picked before saving. If it cannot reach the site, or the folder is not there, you see an error naming the problem — the most common cause is that step 3 has not been completed for this exact site yet, and the **Grant Parle access** button appears alongside the error so you can fix it there and then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Files the agent creates appear in that SharePoint folder; files your team adds there are visible to the agent's tools.

### Granting a site — the easy way

Skip ahead to step 4 and connect the site. When Parle finds it has not been granted access, it offers a **Grant Parle access** button:

- If *you* are a SharePoint or Global Administrator, click it, sign in with Microsoft, and confirm. Come back and press **Resolve** again.
- If you are not, the same button gives you a **link to send to whoever is**. They do not need a Parle account. When they have approved it, press **Resolve** again.

<Info>
  Parle uses that administrator's own sign-in to record the permission and then discards it. It never sees a password, and it gains no access beyond the one site being approved.
</Info>

### Granting a site — the manual way

If the button is not available, or your IT team prefers to script it. You will need **Parle's application ID** — your Parle contact provides this.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="PnP PowerShell">
    ```powershell
    Connect-PnPOnline -Url "https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/ClientDelivery" -Interactive
    Grant-PnPAzureADAppSitePermission `
      -AppId "<Parle application ID>" -DisplayName "Parle" `
      -Site "https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/ClientDelivery" -Permissions Write
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Graph Explorer">
    Open [Graph Explorer](https://developer.microsoft.com/graph/graph-explorer), sign in as an admin, then:

    1. `GET https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/sites/contoso.sharepoint.com:/sites/ClientDelivery` — if prompted, consent to **`Sites.FullControl.All`** on the *Modify permissions* tab, re-run, and copy the site **`id`** from the response.
    2. `POST https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/sites/<site-id>/permissions` with this body. A `201 Created` means it worked:

    ```json
    {
      "roles": ["write"],
      "grantedToIdentities": [
        { "application": { "id": "<Parle application ID>", "displayName": "Parle" } }
      ]
    }
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Tenant reuse:** because you set up single sign-on in Part 1, Parle already knows your tenant — SharePoint connects to the same Microsoft 365 organization automatically, and never asks you for a tenant ID.
</Note>

## SAML single sign-on (Microsoft Entra)

If your Entra directory is set up for SAML 2.0 rather than the OIDC sign-in above, use the **Microsoft SAML single sign-on** card in **Org settings → Organization → Single sign-on**. Microsoft OIDC and Microsoft SAML can both be enabled at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register Parle in Entra">
    The card shows an **Identifier (Entity ID)** and a **Reply URL (Assertion Consumer Service URL)**, unique to your organization. Create an enterprise application in your Entra tenant and enter those two values. You can also show the service provider metadata on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Parle about your directory">
    Enter your **Email domain** and **Microsoft tenant ID**, then give Parle your identity provider's metadata: either an HTTPS **Federation metadata URL** (Parle re-reads it periodically, so a rotated signing certificate keeps working) or the pasted **federation metadata XML** (you must paste it again when the certificate rotates). The identity provider entity ID is optional and normally read from the metadata.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Tick **Enable Microsoft SAML single sign-on** and save. Members are provisioned into your organization on first sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To make SAML or OIDC the only way to sign in, see [Requiring single sign-on](/security/sso#requiring-single-sign-on).

## Using a Microsoft Teams channel

A Teams channel's **Files** tab is just a folder in SharePoint, so pointing a project at a channel is the same flow as above — you just need to know which site the channel lives in. That depends on the channel type:

| Channel type | Where its files live | What to grant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | A folder named after the channel, inside the team's own site (`.../sites/TeamName`), in the **Documents** library | Grant the **team's** site once; every standard channel in that team is then reachable |
| **Private** | Its **own** SharePoint site, typically `.../sites/TeamName-ChannelName` | Grant **that** site separately |
| **Shared** | Its **own** SharePoint site, same naming | Grant **that** site separately |

To find the right site URL, open the channel in Teams → **Files** tab → **⋯ → Open in SharePoint**. Copy the site URL from your browser's address bar — the part up to and including `/sites/<name>`, without the `/Shared Documents/...` tail.

<Warning>
  **A private or shared channel needs its own grant.** It is a separate SharePoint site, so granting the parent team's site does *not* cover it. If verification fails for a private channel, this is almost always why.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Renaming the channel is safe.** Parle binds to the folder itself, not to its name, so renaming a channel — or moving its folder — does not break the project. **Deleting** the folder does: the project's Storage settings will show *"needs reconnecting"*, and an admin re-picks a folder to fix it. Parle deliberately will not fall back to another folder that happens to have the old name, because a new folder reusing that name could belong to a different client.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No **Sign in with Microsoft** button | Domain not configured, or SSO disabled | Check the **email domain** and the **Enable** toggle in Org settings → Organization → Single sign-on |
| Microsoft sign-in succeeds but Parle rejects it | User's email domain does not match a configured organization, or no seats left | Confirm the domain and your available seats |
| Microsoft sign-in unavailable | Plan downgraded below Business | Upgrade, or use email/password |
| **SharePoint** is not offered in a project's storage settings | Not enabled for the org, or plan below Business | Enable it in **Org settings → Files & search → SharePoint**; confirm you are on the **Business** plan or above |
| "SharePoint site not found — check the site URL" | You pasted a *folder* or *library* URL rather than the site's own address | Paste only the part up to and including `/sites/<name>`. Pick the library and folder in the next steps, using the folder browser |
| "Parle does not have access to this SharePoint site" | The site exists, but has not been granted to Parle yet — expected the first time you connect any site | Click **Grant Parle access** on the error, or send the link it gives you to a SharePoint/Global Administrator |
| SharePoint connection fails verification | Site not yet granted to Parle | Complete the per-site grant for that exact site |
| **Grant Parle access** says you lack permission | Granting a site needs **SharePoint Administrator** or **Global Administrator** | Forward the approval link to someone who holds it — the link still works and they need no Parle account |
| Microsoft asks a **Global Administrator** to approve Parle before granting | The first assisted grant in your tenant needs one-time admin consent for Parle's site-access app | Have a Global Administrator complete the sign-in once; afterwards SharePoint Administrators can grant sites themselves |
| Cannot see expected files | Wrong document library or sub-folder selected | Re-check the library/folder in the project's storage settings |
| Connection fails for a **private or shared Teams channel** | That channel is its own SharePoint site and has not been granted | Complete the per-site grant against the channel's **own** site, e.g. `.../sites/TeamName-ChannelName` |
| Project storage says **"needs reconnecting"** | The folder the project pointed at has been deleted, or Parle's access to it was removed | Open **Project settings → Storage** and pick the folder again |

Still stuck? Contact Parle support with your organization name and the site URL you are connecting.
